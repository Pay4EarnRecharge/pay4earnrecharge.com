Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 28-09-2026

1. Overview

At Digital Shop Traders (Operating Brand: Pay4Earn Recharge, Proprietor: Mr. Deepen Kumar Yadav), we strive to deliver reliable mobile recharges, DTH recharges, and bill payment services. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines the process and terms under which transaction refunds are processed.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

A user is eligible for a refund under the following condition:

  • Failed Transaction / Service Not Received: If a payment is successfully debited from your bank account/card/UPI, but the recharge or bill payment failed or was not fulfilled by the telecom operator, you are eligible for a full refund.

3. Non-Refundable Conditions

Refunds will NOT be issued under the following scenarios:

  • Successful Operator Confirmation: If the transaction is marked as successful by the operator and benefits are delivered, the purchase cannot be canceled or refunded.
  • Incorrect Input by User: If an incorrect mobile number, DTH subscriber ID, or wrong operator circle was selected during the transaction and processed successfully.
  • Change of Mind: Cancellation or refund requests due to change of mind after successful completion of recharge.

4. Refund Request Process

If your payment was debited for a failed transaction, please follow these steps:

  • Auto-Refund: Most failed transactions are auto-refunded back to the user's Pay4Earn Recharge wallet or original source account within a few minutes.
  • Manual Query Ticket: If auto-refund does not occur, email our customer support at deepenkumar50@gmail.com with your Order ID, Mobile Number, Transaction Amount, and Payment Reference Number.
  • Verification: Our team verifies the transaction status with the telecom/operator node.

5. Refund Processing Timelines & Mode

Refund Mode: Verified refunds are credited either directly to the user's Pay4Earn Recharge Wallet or refunded to the original payment source (Bank Account / Credit Card / Debit Card / UPI).

Processing Timeline: Wallet credits are instant upon operator confirmation. Payment gateway / source account refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days as per standard banking cycles.
