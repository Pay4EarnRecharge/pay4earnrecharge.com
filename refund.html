Refund & Cancellation Policy

At Pay4Earn Recharge, customer satisfaction is our top priority. Please read our policy regarding failed recharges and refund procedures below.

1. Failed Transactions

If your account or wallet is debited but the mobile/DTH recharge is not delivered, the transaction is marked as failed. In such cases, the debited amount will automatically be credited back to your original payment source or wallet within 5 to 7 business days.

2. Successful Recharges

Once a recharge request is successfully delivered and confirmed by the respective operator (Airtel, Jio, Vi, BSNL, Tata Play, etc.), no cancellation or refund is allowed.

3. Incorrect Number Input

If you enter an incorrect mobile number or subscriber ID and the recharge is processed successfully to that number, Pay4Earn Recharge cannot reverse or refund the transaction.
